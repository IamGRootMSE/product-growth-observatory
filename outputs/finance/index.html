Decision and next investigation

Use the driver bridge to prioritize checks of product mix, discounting and fulfillment cost. Validate those inputs before proposing pricing changes. This fixture cannot identify real customer needs or predict a business outcome.

The generator deliberately shifts mix, price and cost after month 18. This stress-tests forecasting under a regime change; it is not discovered market behavior. Refund timing, multi-item baskets, currencies, acquisition cost, stockouts, subscriptions and customer identity resolution are outside this example.

Run python -m observatory.finance; inspect aggregate evidence and sql/finance.sql. No credentials or cloud service are needed.
